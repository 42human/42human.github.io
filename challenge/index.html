WHAT IT IS

The MindMark Scholars Challenge is an academic assessment program designed to help you understand how prepared you are for New York State tests or the next grade, while motivating students through meaningful rewards and recognition. Each subject—Math, ELA, or Science—is offered as a separate challenge, with its own assessments and prizes.

Awards & Recognition

Grade Bands

Students compete only with peers at the same academic level.

  • Band A: Kindergarten–Grade 1
  • Band B: Grades 2–3
  • Band C: Grades 4–5
  • Band D: Grades 6–8

Winners are selected separately for each subject per grade band.

Prizes

Three winners will be selected for each subject within each grade band.

  • Champion Award $100 + Certificate of Recognition
  • Challenger Award $75 + Certificate of Recognition
  • Commitment Award $50 + Certificate of Recognition

All prizes are awarded after eligibility verification and score review.

What to Know Before You Join

Please review the following before participating

Key Date to Remember

  • All assessments must be completed by March 15, 2026 — 12:00 PM.
  • To ensure fairness for all participants, submissions after this time may not be considered.

Participation Basics

  • Enrollment fees are applied toward participation in the program.
  • By participating, you agree to follow the program guidelines.

Student Verification

  • Enrollment and grade may be confirmed using a school-issued document to ensure fair winner selection.

Winners Announcement and Prize Distribution

  • Winners are typically announced within 7–15 days after the challenge concludes.
  • Prizes are delivered by USPS mail to a confirmed address or through in-person pickup (details shared after verification).

Please note verification is required to confirm eligibility. If verification cannot be completed in time, an alternate winner may be selected. Ties are resolved through a random draw. For complete details, please review our Terms & Conditions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who can participate in the MindMark Scholars Challenge?

Any student in the supported grade bands with a free account.

What subjects are offered?

Math, ELA and full-length test practice across grades 5 to 12, plus SAT.

How does the Challenge work?

Take the challenge test during the window and your score is ranked against your grade band. Top scores earn prizes.

When is the deadline to complete the Challenge?

The window closes at the end of the challenge period shown on the page, so finish before it closes.

Where can students view assessment results?

In your dashboard, under the report for each test.

How are students grouped and ranked?

You are ranked within your own grade and test band, so you are compared with students at the same stage.

How are winners selected?

By score first, with time taken as the tiebreaker.

How much does it cost to participate?

Nothing. Diagnostics, practice tests and the challenge are all free.

How are prizes delivered?

Prizes are sent digitally to the email on your account once results are verified.

Who can I contact if I need help?

Use the contact page — messages reach a real inbox and usually get a reply within a day.
